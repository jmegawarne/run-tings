Daily

Your morning

Before you leave the house.

  1. Bed made.
  2. Pyjamas folded or hung up.
  3. Yesterday's clothes in your wash basket or put away.
  4. Window open while you get ready, shut before you go.
  5. Breakfast things, and any cups or plates from your room, in the dishwasher.
  6. PE day? PE kit packed.
  7. Phone, wallet, and keys on you.

Done when: bed made, floor clear, window shut, and no dishes in your room.

Coming home

As soon as you're home for the day.

  1. Shoes and coat in their place in the living room, and slippers on.
  2. Hands washed.
  3. Lunch box and water bottle in the dishwasher.
  4. Water bottle in the dishwasher.
  5. Bag upstairs.
  6. Out of uniform: shirt in your wash basket, and the rest hung up.
  7. PE day? PE kit in your wash basket.
  8. Windows open, if the weather's right.
  9. Dinner started, then a short break.
  10. Homework: real progress made before anything else.

Done when: coat, shoes, bag, and uniform away, slippers on, and dinner or homework under way.

Your evening

Before bed.

  1. Your things off the coffee table and sofas.
  2. Shoes and coat in their place in the living room.
  3. Bag, clothes, and charger upstairs.
  4. Cups and plates in the dishwasher.
  5. Phone charging on a hard surface, not on the bed.
  6. Showered. A quick one counts.

Done when: nothing of yours left downstairs, no dishes upstairs, and you've showered.

After every shower

Downstairs shower, every time.

  1. Glass and walls squeegeed.
  2. Leave-on shower spray on.
  3. Hair out of the plughole.
  4. Towel on the rail or your hook.
  5. Bath mat on the lower radiator rung.
  6. Fan on or window open.

Done when: glass squeegeed and sprayed, plughole clear, and towel and mat hung up.

Folding and putting away

Every day, as soon as it's dry.

  1. Your dry laundry off the airer and racks.
  2. Folded or hung up, and put away.

Done when: none of your dry laundry left on the airer or racks, and it's all put away.

Dishwasher

Every morning, before you leave.

  1. Dishwasher emptied and everything put away.

Done when: the dishwasher is empty and ready for the day's dishes.

Weekday dinner

Monday to Friday, together. Dinner at 7pm.

Before you start

  1. Tonight's meal and who does what agreed.
  2. Dishwasher empty, sink empty, and worktops clear.
  3. Mise en place: every ingredient prepped, and every pan and tool out, before the heat goes on.

On time

  1. Started at the time that gets dinner out at 7pm.
  2. Everyone told 10 minutes before.
  3. Dinner on the table at 7pm.

  2. Food scraps in the kitchen bin.
  3. Dishwasher loaded and on.
  4. Pans, utensils, and anything that can't go in the dishwasher washed, dried, and put away.

  8. Kitchen bin full? Out to the outside bin, and a new bag in.

How it works

  • Your jobs. Pick your name at the top. You'll see your own jobs, this week's zone, and anything due today.
  • Zones. Zones rotate every Monday, so everyone learns every job. On weekdays, Aurora and Eshaal swap between Kitchen and Bathrooms, and Living & outside. At the weekend, Aurora, Eshaal, and Leo rotate through all three. Leo's weekday evenings stay free, and floors and dusting are always his at the weekend. Weekday dinners are always Aurora and Eshaal's.
  • The standard. "Done when" is the standard. If it isn't met, it isn't done.
  • Ticks. Ticks stay on your phone and nobody else sees them. They clear themselves overnight, on Mondays, and when the month or quarter turns over.
  • Your own dishes. Straight into the dishwasher, every time.
  • Running low. Used the last of something? Tell Aurora before Friday's shop.
  • Meter readings. When the energy company emails for them, Dad passes it to Aurora.
  • Swaps. Agree a swap with the other person before the day, then tell Dad.
  • Broken or leaking. Tell Dad the same day. If you smell gas, get out first, then call 0800 111 999.
  • A better way. Tell Dad. If it's better, the page changes.

Stages

  1. Learn. Follow the list. One reminder per job is fine.
  2. Own. Nobody reminds you. Your jobs happen at their time.
  3. Run. You don't need the list any more.

Target: everyone at Own by 1 February 2027. Dad's Saturday walk-rounds stop for each person when they reach Own.

Safety rules

  1. Never mix bleach with anything except water. Mixed with limescale remover or other cleaners, it gives off a toxic gas.
  2. Use the step stool, never a chair.
  3. Unplug anything electrical before you clean it.
  4. Candles are Dad's job: he lights them and puts them out.
  5. Hob and oven off before you leave the kitchen.

When the cleaner starts

The cleaner takes the weekly zone cleans and most of the monthly lists. Daily jobs, dinners, laundry, bedrooms, bins, and the shop stay with us. A cleaner can only clean what they can get to, so tidy before they arrive.
